Price and Charges

Price

The "Premium" paid plan is offered as a monthly and an annual subscription. The price of each plan (tax included), the plans currently available, and the availability and length of any introductory free trial are as shown on the in-app purchase screen and the App Store listing. Prices differ by country and region and may change due to exchange rates, store fees, or tax revisions, so they are not reproduced on this page; the amount displayed at the time of purchase applies.

Scope of the Plan

The App is designed for use by two people (a couple). If either member of a pair subscribes to Premium, both members can use the paid features. There is no need to purchase two subscriptions.

Additional Charges

Internet and mobile data charges required to use and download the App are the responsibility of the user.

Payment

Payment Method

Payment is made through the in-app purchase system provided by the Apple App Store. We do not collect or retain users' credit card or other payment details.

Payment Timing

You are charged at the time of purchase via the App Store. Unless cancelled, subscriptions renew automatically on the renewal date defined by the App Store and are charged at the same amount. Where a free trial applies, you are charged at the end of the trial period.

Delivery / Service Availability

Upon completion of payment, the paid plan (Premium features) is activated and becomes available immediately. Premium features become available on the partner's side as soon as synchronization completes.

Cancellation, Returns, and Refunds

  • Subscriptions can be stopped at any time from your App Store account settings ("Subscriptions"). Cancelling before the renewal date stops the next automatic renewal and charge.
  • After cancellation, Premium features remain available until the end of the period already paid for. No pro-rata refunds are provided for cancellation mid-period as a rule.
  • When the subscriber cancels, the partner's access to Premium features also ends at the close of the period already paid for.
  • Due to the nature of digital content (services), cancellations or returns at the user's own convenience after purchase cannot be accepted. Whether a refund is possible and how to request it are governed by Apple's refund policy.

Operating Environment

The App is provided for iOS. The operating environment (supported OS versions, etc.) follows the description on the App Store listing page.
