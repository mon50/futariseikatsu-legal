When reporting a bug, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. You can also write to us from Settings → About this app → Contact us in the app.

FAQ

How do we connect as partners?

One of you sends an invite with "Send invite link", and the other opens the link or enters the invite code. Invite codes expire after 72 hours.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription automatically.

Premium disappeared after changing phones or reinstalling

Tap "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen in the app.

What happens when I log out?

Your content is removed from this device. Log in again with the same Apple Account to get back the content you had sent. If some changes haven't reached your partner yet, the app tells you before logging out.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account management → Delete account. Deleted data cannot be restored, even if you log in again with the same Apple Account.
