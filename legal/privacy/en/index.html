FutariSeikatsu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27 ・ Effective: 2026-07-27
ja| en

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how FutariSeikatsu (the "App") handles information about its users (the "User"). The App is developed and operated by an individual ("we" / "us").

The App lets two people create "topics to discuss," write down their own views, and record the decisions they reach. Because of this design, what you enter is shared with the partner you are paired with and is stored on cloud infrastructure operated by our processors so that both devices stay in sync. See §3 and §4 for details.

The Japanese version of this Policy is the authoritative original. In the event of any conflict between the Japanese and English versions, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Authentication: using the App requires signing in with Sign in with Apple. Our authentication backend (Supabase Auth, see §3.1) receives and stores the user identifier and the email address provided by Apple. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email," what we receive is the private relay address issued by Apple, and your actual email address is not disclosed to us.
  • Profile: the nickname and avatar type you set inside the App. We never ask for your legal name; you may set any nickname you like.

1.2 Pairing Information

  • The identifier of the couple (the pair of two accounts), the invitation code, the pairing status, and which accounts belong to it.

1.3 Content You Create

The following content you enter is stored on the server (see §3.2):

  • Topics (events): title, chosen icon, template, and progress status.
  • Discussion fields: name, type, and options.
  • The opinions, preferences, and stances you enter for each field.
  • Decisions: the decided value, who decided it, and the method used.
  • Comments, reactions, and replies to comments.
  • Images you optionally attach as a reaction. The photo library is accessed only when you use this feature, and only the image you select is handled. Attached images are stored on the server and are visible to your partner.

This content may include descriptions of your and your partner's life and future plans. We do not restrict what you may enter, but we recommend that you avoid entering sensitive information you do not need to record (such as beliefs, medical history, or criminal records).

1.4 Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase the "Premium" paid plan, the payment itself is processed by Apple's in-app purchase system (StoreKit). We store your purchase status (the identifier of the purchased plan, whether the subscription is active, and its expiry) on the server. This is necessary so that when one member of a couple subscribes, the other can also use the paid features; your purchase status is therefore also readable by your partner. We do not collect or retain your credit card number or any other payment details.

1.5 Push Notification Information

If you allow push notifications, we store the device token issued by Apple Push Notification service (APNs) on the server. It is used solely to notify you of your partner's entries and decisions. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

1.6 Information Stored On Your Device

  • A local cache (an on-device database) used for offline display and synchronization.
  • App preferences such as display language, sign-in state, onboarding-completed flag, and notification settings.

1.7 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / Advertising ID). The App shows no advertising.
  • Location, contacts, microphone, or camera access. (The photo library is accessed only in the case described in §1.3.)
  • Crash reports or analytics SDKs (Firebase / Crashlytics / Google Analytics, etc.).
  • The App performs no generative-AI processing and never sends your input to an AI service.
  • Postal address, phone number, date of birth, gender, or credit card information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • To share, synchronize, and deliver real-time updates of content between the two of you.
  • To determine paid-plan entitlement for both members of a couple.
  • To send push notifications about your partner's activity.
  • To prevent abuse and to respond to your inquiries.

We do not use any information for machine-learning training, advertising, profiling, sale to third parties, or any other secondary purpose. We do not read the content you create, except where required to respond to a lawful disclosure request or where you ask us to investigate a problem.

Legal basis (GDPR): the processing above is necessary for the performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and for our legitimate interest in preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)). Push notifications rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time.

3. Third-Party Processors and International Transfers

3.1 Processors

We engage the following third parties as processors. They act on our behalf and do not receive your information as independent recipients.

  • Supabase, Inc. (United States)
    • Purpose: user authentication (Supabase Auth), database, real-time synchronization, and image storage.
    • Information sent: all of the information described in §1.1 through §1.5.
    • We operate our database in a Supabase cloud region (in the United States or a comparable region).
  • Apple Inc. (United States)
    • Purpose: authentication via Sign in with Apple, in-app purchase (payment and subscription management), and push notification delivery (APNs).
    • Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third party (advertisers, data brokers, or external companies).

3.2 International Transfers

The processors above are located in the United States, and your information is stored on servers outside Japan. Supabase and Apple each apply safeguards for personal information under their respective privacy policies and data processing terms, including Standard Contractual Clauses. Please refer to their privacy policies for details.

4. Sharing With Your Partner

The App is designed to be used by two people. While a pairing is active, the following is visible to your partner. This is a core function of the App and cannot be disabled item by item.

  • Your nickname and avatar type.
  • The topics, fields, opinions, decisions, comments, and attached images you create or enter (everything in §1.3).
  • Your paid-plan status (active or not — this does not include payment details or amounts).

Your partner can edit and delete shared topics and fields. In contrast, the opinions and comments you post can, as a rule, only be deleted by you.

We cannot take responsibility for how information shared between the two of you is subsequently used. Do not enter anything you do not want to share. If you end the pairing, information created afterwards is no longer shared with the other person, but we cannot retrieve information that was already displayed on or saved to their device.

5. Storage Locations and Retention

5.1 Server Side

  • Account information, pairing information, and the content you create are retained until you delete your account.
  • We do not unilaterally set a retention period after which data is deleted.
  • Device tokens are deleted when you turn off notifications or delete your account.

5.2 On-Device Data

  • The local cache and app preferences are removed from your device when you sign out or uninstall the App.

6. Security

  • Encryption in transit: all traffic between your device and the server uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Row-level security (RLS): every database table enforces row-level access control, so an authenticated account can only read and write data belonging to itself and to its own couple. Unauthenticated access is rejected entirely.
  • Authentication: authentication relies on Sign in with Apple; we never handle passwords. Protection on the device side depends on your device lock (passcode / biometrics) and the operating system's disk encryption. We strongly recommend enabling a passcode or biometric lock and keeping your OS up to date.
  • Least privilege: operations requiring administrative rights (such as account deletion) run only through a restricted server-side function and cannot be invoked directly by the App.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your information:

  • Access: you can review the data you created and your profile from within the App. You may also request disclosure of the personal information we hold using the contact in §9.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account at any time via "Settings → Account Management." Deletion removes, from the server, your profile, subscription status, device tokens, the opinions and comments you posted, and any couple you created together with its topics, fields, and decisions; your authentication record (the account itself) is deleted as well. If you had joined a couple created by your partner, you simply leave that couple and their data remains theirs. This action cannot be undone.
  • Correction: you can edit your profile (such as your nickname) and the content you entered from within the App.
  • Restriction / objection: you can stop push notifications in your device settings, and stop sharing with your partner by ending the pairing.
  • Complaints: contact us using the address in §9 with any complaint regarding this Policy or our handling of your information.
  • Regulatory complaints: EU / UK users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority; users in Japan may contact the Personal Information Protection Commission.

Do-not-sell declaration (CCPA): we do not sell the personal information of any User, including California residents.
